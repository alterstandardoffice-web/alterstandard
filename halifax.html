01 — Design Notes

A city in ocean blue

Halifax has been a working harbor longer than Canada has been a country. We built the line around ocean blue — the color of the Atlantic on a clear day off the waterfront, not the postcard navy every port town reaches for.

The mark holds the same restraint as the rest of the collection: a condensed serif, set with the plainspoken confidence of a harbor sign. No lighthouse shorthand, no anchor cliché — just the city's name, in a blue that means something to anyone who's watched the fog roll off the Atlantic.

A small line built to travel: a tee to start, made to be given as easily as it's kept — a piece of the East Coast for anyone who's ever called it home.
