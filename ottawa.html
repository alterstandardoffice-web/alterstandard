01 — Design Notes

A capital in deep navy

Ottawa doesn't need to raise its voice. It's the seat of government, so we built the line around a deep navy instead of a parliamentary red or a maple leaf shorthand — the color of the Gothic Revival stone on Parliament Hill after dark, and of the Rideau Canal on a still winter night before the skaters arrive.

The mark carries the same restraint as the rest of the collection: a condensed serif set with the quiet formality of a government letterhead. No Peace Tower cliché, no hockey-on-ice shorthand — just the city's name, in a navy that means something to anyone who's called this bilingual, unshowy capital home.

A small, deliberate line, growing the same way the rest of the collection did: a sticker to start, more to follow — a quiet way to carry a piece of the capital, wherever you're wearing it.
