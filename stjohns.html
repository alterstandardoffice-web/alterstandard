01 — Design Notes

A city in jellybean row

St. John's is the oldest city on the continent, and it wears that history in color instead of stone. We built the line around the row houses on Gower and Duckworth Streets — jelly bean red, heritage mustard, harbor blue, set against a foggy Atlantic sky. It's the one collection in the series that doesn't restrain itself to a single tone, because the city itself doesn't either.

The mark keeps the same quiet confidence as the rest of the collection: a condensed serif set the way you'd see it painted above a shopfront on Water Street. No puffin shorthand, no jelly bean cliché — just the city's name, in colors that mean something to anyone who's walked downhill into the harbor fog.

A small, deliberate line, growing the same way the rest of the collection did: a sticker to start, more to follow — a quiet way to carry a piece of the rock, wherever you're wearing it.
