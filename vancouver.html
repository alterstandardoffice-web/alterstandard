01 — Design Notes

A city in forest green

Vancouver is the one Canadian city where the wilderness never actually leaves — it's visible from the window of every glass tower downtown. We built the line around forest green instead of the ocean blues most coastal cities default to, because the mountains are the first thing anyone here points to, not the water.

The mark keeps the same restraint as the rest of the collection: a condensed serif set the way you'd label a trailhead, not a tourist shop window. No totem-pole shorthand, no rain-cloud cliché — just the city's name, in the color of the rainforest it's built into.

A small line for a city that layers a raincoat over everything: a tee to start, built to be given as easily as it's kept.
