01 — Design Notes

A territory in gold

Yukon doesn't need embellishing. It's the one line in the collection built around gold instead of a blue or green — the color of the aurora catching low light, and of the river that drew people north over a century ago. It's a color that has to be earned by what it's put on, not the other way around.

The mark keeps the same restraint as the rest of the collection: a condensed serif set with the plainspoken confidence of a highway sign at the edge of the territory. No pickaxe shorthand, no husky cliché — just the name, in a color that means something to anyone who's watched northern lights from a driveway in February.

A small, deliberate line, growing the same way the rest of the collection did: a sticker to start, more to follow — a quiet way to carry a piece of the North, wherever you're wearing it.
